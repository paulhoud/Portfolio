## À propos de ce site

Ce site est mon portfolio : une vitrine de mon travail de Product Designer, pensée aussi comme un terrain de jeu.

## About this site

This site is my portfolio: a showcase of my work as a Product Designer, also built as a playground.

As a kid of the 90s, I filled it with references to the world I grew up with: video games, cartoons, toys and everyday objects from that era.

## References and rights

Elements that belong to their creators (characters, logos, music, sounds, images or 3D models tied to well-known works) are nods. They are first and foremost easter eggs, hidden for those who will recognise them.
